=== PSYCHOMETRIC TEST INTERPRETATION & SCORING NORMS CORPUS ===

1. PATIENT HEALTH QUESTIONNAIRE (PHQ-9):
- Cut-offs: 0-4 None/Minimal; 5-9 Mild; 10-14 Moderate; 15-19 Moderately Severe; 20-27 Severe.
- Clinical significance: Score >= 10 indicates high sensitivity and specificity for Major Depressive Disorder.
- Critical Item 9: Any score > 0 on thoughts of death or self-harm triggers immediate compassionate crisis safety screening.

2. GENERALIZED ANXIETY DISORDER SCALE (GAD-7):
- Cut-offs: 0-4 Minimal anxiety; 5-9 Mild anxiety; 10-14 Moderate anxiety; 15-21 Severe anxiety.
- Cut-off of 10 represents optimal threshold for identifying Generalized Anxiety Disorder, Panic Disorder, and Social Anxiety Disorder.

3. TRIARCHIC PSYCHOPATHY MODEL (TriPM):
- Boldness: Social poise, stress resilience, venturesomeness. High boldness in the absence of meanness/disinhibition correlates with successful leadership, heroism, and emotional stability.
- Meanness: Callousness, predatory exploitation, cruelty. Pathognomonic marker for interpersonal cruelty and lack of empathetic concern.
- Disinhibition: Impulsivity, impatience, alienation, externalizing blame. Strongly associated with substance misuse, criminal recidivism, and behavioral dysregulation.

4. LEVENSON SELF-REPORT PSYCHOPATHY (LSRP):
- Primary Factor (Interpersonal/Affective): Cold, manipulative, remorseless selfishness. Low neuroticism, autonomic under-arousal.
- Secondary Factor (Antisocial/Lifestyle): Erratically impulsive, aggressive, quick-tempered, emotionally unstable, hostile worldview. High neuroticism.

5. SHORT DARK TRIAD (SD3):
- Machiavellianism: Strategic cynicism, political calculation, deception.
- Narcissism: Grandiose vanity, entitlement, admiration craving.
- Psychopathy: Impulsive thrill-seeking, callous affect, empathy deficits.

6. BIG FIVE / OCEAN-30:
- Openness: Fantasy, Aesthetics, Feelings, Actions, Ideas, Values.
- Conscientiousness: Competence, Order, Dutifulness, Achievement Striving, Self-Discipline, Deliberation.
- Extraversion: Warmth, Gregariousness, Assertiveness, Activity, Excitement Seeking, Positive Emotions.
- Agreeableness: Trust, Straightforwardness, Altruism, Compliance, Modesty, Tender-Mindedness.
- Neuroticism: Anxiety, Angry Hostility, Depression, Self-Consciousness, Impulsiveness, Vulnerability.

7. ADULT ATTACHMENT DIMENSIONS (ECR):
- Low Anxiety, Low Avoidance: Secure Attachment (Comfortable with intimacy and autonomy).
- High Anxiety, Low Avoidance: Anxious-Preoccupied Attachment (Desperate for validation, hypersensitive to rejection).
- Low Anxiety, High Avoidance: Dismissive-Avoidant Attachment (Compulsively self-reliant, suppress emotions).
- High Anxiety, High Avoidance: Fearful-Avoidant / Disorganized (Craves intimacy but terrified of betrayal).
