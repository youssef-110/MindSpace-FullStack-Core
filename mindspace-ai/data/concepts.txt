Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT): A form of psychological treatment that focuses on changing patterns of thinking or behavior that are behind people's difficulties.
Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR): An evidence-based program that offers secular, intensive mindfulness training to assist people with stress, anxiety, and depression.
The ABC Model: Used in therapy to understand the relationship between Activating events (A), Beliefs (B), and Consequences (C).
