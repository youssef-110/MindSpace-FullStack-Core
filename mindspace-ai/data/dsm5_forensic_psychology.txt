DIFFERENTIAL ANALYSIS: PSYCHOPATHY VS. SOCIOPATHY VS. NARCISSISM
1. Psychopathy (Triarchic Model - Dr. Christopher Patrick):
- Defined across three phenotypic dimensions: Boldness, Meanness, and Disinhibition.
- Boldness: Characterized by low fear, social dominance, high thrill-seeking threshold, stress immunity, and autonomic hypo-reactivity (reduced amygdala activation to threat cues).
- Meanness: Callous disregard for others, deficient empathy, instrumental exploitation, cynical arrogance, and inability to form authentic emotional attachments.
- Disinhibition: Impulsive behavioral control deficits, low frustration tolerance, impaired delayed gratification, and sensation-seeking without foresight.
- Neurobiological underpinnings: Structural and functional anomalies in the paralimbic system, fronto-striatal pathways, and ventromedial prefrontal cortex (vmPFC).

2. Sociopathy / Secondary Psychopathy (Levenson LSRP & DSM-5 ASPD):
- Primary Psychopathy: Emotional coldness, calculated deceit, lack of remorse, egocentricity. Often innate temperament with autonomic hypo-arousal.
- Secondary Psychopathy (Sociopathy): High impulsivity, reactive aggression, hostile attribution bias, severe emotional turbulence, often stemming from severe childhood adversity, inconsistent attachment, or environmental trauma. Unlike primary psychopathy, secondary psychopathy experiences high anxiety and emotional distress.
- DSM-5-TR Antisocial Personality Disorder (ASPD): Pervasive pattern of disregard for and violation of the rights of others occurring since age 15, evidenced by failure to conform to lawful norms, deceitfulness, impulsivity, irritability and aggressiveness, reckless disregard for safety, and lack of remorse.

3. Narcissistic Personality Constellation (NPI-16 & DSM-5-TR NPD):
- Core feature: Pervasive pattern of grandiosity (in fantasy or behavior), need for continuous admiration, and lack of empathy.
- Sub-types:
  * Grandiose Narcissism: Overt arrogance, exhibitionism, high extraversion, thick-skinned sense of entitlement, self-assured exploitation.
  * Vulnerable / Covert Narcissism: Hypersensitivity to rejection or criticism, deep underlying shame, introverted neuroticism, quiet resentment, and defensive passive-aggression.
- Differentiation from Psychopathy: Narcissists care deeply about public perception, social standing, and validation, experiencing narcissistic injury when slighted. Psychopaths are indifferent to public opinion unless it directly impacts instrumental goals.

4. The Dark Triad (SD3 Model):
- Machiavellianism: Strategic cynicism, long-term manipulative calculation ("the ends justify the means"), alliance-building for leverage, emotional detachment without overt impulsivity.
- Narcissism: Self-aggrandizement, demand for prestige, entitlement.
- Subclinical Psychopathy: Immediate thrill-seeking, impulsivity, thrill from rule-breaking, callous disregard for consequences.
